计算机科学与技术专业（自然语言处理方向）硕博连读研究生，热爱机器翻译和条件计算相关研究，熟练掌握 Shell、Python 语言编程，开源框架 Fairseq、Transformers(Hugging Face)，以及 LaTex 编程，对动态网络结构以及大模型高效推断十分感兴趣。希望在未来可以从事大模型文本生成、多模态大模型，以及以条件计算（早退和混合专家）为基础的大模型建模以及大模型推断效率方向的研究工作。

PhD student in Computer Science and Technology (Natural Language Processing). Passionate about research in machine translation, audio processing and conditional computation. Proficient in Shell and Python programming, as well as open-source frameworks such as Fairseq, Transformers (Hugging Face), and LaTeX programming. Highly interested in dynamic network structures and efficient inference in large language models. I hope to pursue research about text generation, multimodal models, as well as large model modeling and inference efficiency based on conditional computation (early exit and mixture of experts) in the future.
